About us

A warm table in the heart of Lapaz.

Royal Delight Cafe and Restaurant is a food and hospitality business at Tabora No. 3, Lapaz, Accra. We serve delicious, high-quality meals and beverages in a welcoming setting, and we want every guest to leave with a good memory.

Our vision

Ghana's leading dining destination, and beyond.

To become the leading dining destination in Ghana and beyond, setting the standard for excellence in food, hospitality, and customer experience while creating lasting memories for every guest.

Our mission

Warmth, elegance and care on every plate.

To serve delicious, high-quality meals and beverages in an environment that reflects warmth, elegance, and care, exceeding customer expectations through exceptional service, innovation, integrity, and a passion for hospitality.
